EXPERIENCE

Discover the Fringe

For three weeks in August, the city of Edinburgh welcomes an explosion of creative energy from around the globe. Artists and performers take to hundreds of stages all over the city, presenting shows for every taste - from big names in the world of entertainment to unknown artists looking to build their careers.

Learn more

TAKE PART

Take part in the Fringe

Anyone can take part in the Fringe. We work to make the Fringe an open, accessible and welcoming festival to all. That’s why we offer a wide range of resources, practical advice and step-by-step how-to guides designed to support you be part of the greatest platform for creative freedom in the world.

Learn more

Support our work

The Fringe Society, a registered charity, depends on support from people like you. Your generosity allows us to continue our work: supporting artists, assisting audiences, and celebrating the Fringe and what it stands for all over the world. Every donation makes a difference.
