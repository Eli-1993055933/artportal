Open Calls

About

More

Whether we’re honoring achievements in media art or offering exciting residency opportunities, Ars Electronica and its partners are always on the lookout for creative minds who want to present their work to a wider audience or draw inspiration for their work from other institutions. The following Open Calls are currently accepting submissions:

Prix Ars Electronica 2027 Submission start: January 2027
Win the coveted Golden Nica and prize money of up to 10,000 euros plus a prominent appearance at the Ars Electronica Festival!

Prix Ars Electronica u19 - create your world 2027 Submission deadline: March 2027
For all Young Creatives (0-14 years) and Young Professionals (14-19 years) in Austria: You can submit your projects and ideas of tomorrow for the 2027 competition starting from June 2026 until March, 2027!
