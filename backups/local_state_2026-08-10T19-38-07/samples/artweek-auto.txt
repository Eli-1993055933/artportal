Singapore Art Week
The Singapore Art Week (SAW) 2026 season may be over but we can't wait to do it all again with you next year. See you at SAW 2027, from 21 - 31 Jan.
List with us in the SAW 2027 Publicity Form !
For the latest, follow @sgartweek on Facebook and Instagram .

Singapore Art Museum
