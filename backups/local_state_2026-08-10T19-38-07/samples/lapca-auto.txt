摘要: Casa México made its Los Angeles debut with five sold-out live viewing parties, cultural programming, and art exhibitions. LOS ANGELES (July 23, 2026) – More than 13,000 people celebrated Mexico's participation in the World Cup at Casa México Los Angeles 2026, presented by the Consulate General of Mexico in Los Angeles, LA Plaza de Cultura y Artes, and Telemundo 52. The official hospitality venue of the Government of Mexico will conclude its successful six-week run on July 19. Starting on June 11, Casa México became one of Los Angeles' premier destinations for fans following the Mexican National Team throughout the tournament. Free tickets for each of the five free live viewing parties sold out within days—and later, within minutes—reflecting the overwhelming enthusiasm of the community. "Casa México Los Angeles demonstrated the extraordinary power of soccer to bring people together while celebrating the richness of Mexican culture. We are deeply grateful to the thousands of families and visitors who joined us throughout the tournament. At a time when North America welcomed the world, Casa México proudly showcased the warmth, creativity, and diversity that define Mexico and the vibrant Mexican community that calls this city home," said Consul General Carlos González Gutiérrez. This marks the first time Casa México, the Government of Mexico's official World Cup hospitality venue, has been presented in Los Angeles, the city with the largest population of people of Mexican origin outside of Mexico. The Casa México concept has previously been presented by the Government of Mexico at major international events, including the South by Southwest (SXSW) festival in Austin (2016-2018) and during San Diego Comic-Con (2022 and 2023), where it showcased Mexican culture, innovation, tourism, and creative industries. More than a place to watch soccer, Casa México offered visitors an immersive cultural experience celebrating the richness and diversity of Mexico. Through art exhibitions, live music and dance performances, regional cuisine, and family-friendly activities, the program showcased the traditions and cultural heritage of different Mexican states while bringing communities together through the language of soccer. "Casa México welcomed not only Mexican and Mexican American communities from across the Los Angeles region but also visitors from Latino, Asian, Black, and Afro-Latino communities who came together to celebrate the World Cup alongside Mexico's supporters," said Tracy Serrano, Interim CEO of LA Plaza de Cultura y Artes. "Bringing together such a diverse audience is a remarkable achievement for our institution, especially as we celebrate our 15th anniversary." Hosted at LA Plaza de Cultura y Artes, the downtown Los Angeles museum dedicated to telling the story of Mexicans, Mexican Americans, and the Latino experience in Los Angeles, Casa México drew more than 10,000 attendees to its five Live Viewing Parties. Following Mexico's elimination from the tournament, nearly 2,000 additional guests participated in two closing celebrations: a Summer of Salsa concert featuring La Verdad and Super DJ Robby and a Mariachi Family Festival showcasing performances by three local student ensembles. 标题: Soccer meets art at Casa México
链接: https://lapca.org/soccer-meets-art-at-casa-mexico/?utm_source=rss&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=soccer-meets-art-at-casa-mexico
日期: Mon, 06 Jul 2026 16:29:15 +0000
摘要: Through contemporary Mexican art, "Goles de Pintura" by Jazzamoart and "Tejiendo Redes: Uniendo comunidades a través del arte y el fútbol" by Betsabeé Romero explore the passion, history, and cultural significance of the world's most beloved sport. LOS ANGELES (July 6, 2026)—Soccer isn't only played on the field at Casa México, it also comes alive on its walls. Colorfully painted soccer balls, monumental portraits of players, and a sculptural goal featuring contemporary soccer players interwoven alongside Mesoamerican ballplayers are among the artistic experiences visitors can enjoy at Casa México, in LA Plaza de Cultura y Artes, throughout the remainder of the World Cup. Through contemporary Mexican art, Goles de Pintura by Jazzamoart and Tejiendo Redes: Uniendo comunidades a través del arte y el fútbol by Betsabeé Romero explore the passion, history, and cultural significance of the world's most beloved sport. As the official home of the government of Mexico during the World Cup, Casa México has already welcomed nearly 10,000 fans who have gathered to cheer on the Mexican national team. Beyond the excitement of the matches, visitors have the opportunity to experience art inspired by soccer and cultural exchange. "Soccer has an extraordinary ability to unite people across generations, cultures, and borders. Art does the same," said Carlos González Gutiérrez, Consul General of Mexico in Los Angeles. "At Casa México, we're bringing those two powerful forces together to celebrate the creativity, history, and cultural identity that define Mexico. We want every visitor to leave with unforgettable memories of what they discovered through art." Goles de Pintura (“Painted Goals”) showcases a vibrant collection of paintings and hand-painted soccer balls inspired by the passion, rhythm, and emotion of the beautiful game. The artworks are located on the 1st and 2nd floors of the museum, and Romero’s public art installation is located in LA Plaza’s courtyard. A native of Irapuato, Guanajuato, Jazzamoart first explored the intersection of soccer, Jazz, and visual art in 1986, when he participated in a group exhibition organized by Mexico's National Institute of Fine Arts during the World Cup held in Mexico. Throughout his career, he has worked across multiple artistic disciplines including sculpture in wood, marble, bronze, and ceramics, as well as drawing, printmaking, object art, and installation. Large-scale oil painting remains his signature medium. Through bold colors and expressive brushstrokes, his work captures the energy, movement, emotion, and connection shared by both music and sports. Tejiendo Redes: Uniendo comunidades a través del arte y el fútbol (Weaving Nets: Uniting Communities Through Art and Soccer) by renowned Mexican artist Betsabeé Romero, is a participatory art installation that brings migrant communities together through collective creation. Developed through community workshops, the project takes the form of a sculptural soccer goal woven with textiles created by migrants. Metal figures representing both Mesoamerican ballplayers and contemporary soccer players are intertwined throughout the structure, creating a powerful dialogue between past and present, migration and belonging, art and sport. The binational project celebrates shared identity and the ties that connect communities across borders. 摘要: Los Angeles, CA (May 7, 2025) –– LA Plaza de Cultura y Artes is undergoing a leadership transition and has taken a series of steps to strengthen the organization’s long-term sustainability and continued service to the community. In recent months, the Board has taken thoughtful steps to stabilize operations, align resources with institutional priorities, and position LA Plaza for the future. These actions include leadership changes, organizational adjustments, and a careful review of expenses to ensure the organization remains strong and mission focused. “These decisions were made with great care and deep sense of responsibility to the institution and the community we serve,” said Board Chair Robert Villaseñor. “Our focus is on ensuring that LA Plaza remains a vibrant, sustainable cultural anchor for Los Angeles for years to come.” Tracy M. Serrano-Ruiz, Interim Chief Executive Officer As part of this transition, the Board has appointed Tracy M. Serrano-Ruiz as Interim Chief Executive Officer, effective immediately. A respected and steady leader, Serrano-Ruiz has served as Chief of Staff and has been instrumental in strengthening internal operations, governance, and mission delivery over many years. “The Board has full confidence in Tracy’s leadership during this time,” Villaseñor added. “She brings deep institutional knowledge, a strong commitment to our mission, and the steady leadership needed to guide LA Plaza forward.” “I am honored to step into this role and continue serving an institution that means so much to our community,” said Serrano-Ruiz. “LA Plaza holds a vital place in preserving and sharing our stories, and I look forward to collaborating with our staff, Board, and partners to build on that legacy and a strong future.” With these actions and new leadership in place, LA Plaza is well-positioned to move forward with clarity, stability, and renewed focus. The organization remains committed to delivering high-quality cultural programming and to celebrating the enduring contributions of Mexicans, Mexican Americans, and all Latinos in Los Angeles. 标题: Casa México Los Ángeles Announces its Calendar of Activities during the World Cup
链接: https://lapca.org/casa-mexico-los-angeles/?utm_source=rss&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=casa-mexico-los-angeles
日期: Tue, 05 May 2026 18:53:35 +0000
摘要: JOINT STATEMENT Activities include live viewing parties of the Mexican National Team's first three matches, two family festivals, representations of various states of the Mexican Republic, and more. LOS ANGELES (May 5, 2026) – Casa México Los Ángeles 2026, the official hospitality venue of the Government of Mexico during the World Cup, will host live viewing parties for the first three matches of the Mexican National Football Team, two Family Festivals and a salsa concert, among other activities; this was announced on Tuesday as the details of the programming to be offered during the World Cup—which will span just over five weeks—were unveiled. Based at LA Plaza de Cultura y Artes in downtown Los Angeles, Casa México Los Ángeles 2026 will kick off its activities on June 11 with a live broadcast—shown on a giant screen—of the Mexican National Team's opening match against South Africa, taking place in Mexico City. Subsequently, it will screen the matches on June 18 against South Korea and on June 24 against the Czech Republic. Depending on the Mexican National Team's performance in the tournament, Casa México will announce additional screenings or other cultural activities. “Casa México Los Ángeles 2026 will be a gathering space to proudly celebrate our roots, support the Mexican National Team, and share the cultural richness of our country with all communities in Southern California. We want every visitor to feel at home and experience a true celebration of unity,” shared the Consul General of Mexico in Los Angeles, Carlos González Gutiérrez. In addition to screening the matches, Casa México will host two Family Days. The first of these community events will take place on Sunday, June 28, and the second on Saturday, July 11. The latter will feature performances by three youth mariachi bands. The Family Days—part of LA Plaza’s signature programming—will offer activities, workshops, and entertainment for all ages. Likewise, attendees will be able to enjoy music and the presence of various states of Mexico, showcasing their culture, cuisine, and tourism offerings. “LA Plaza, an institution dedicated to sharing the diverse stories of Mexicans, Mexican Americans, and Latinos in Los Angeles, is honored to become Casa México during the World Cup,” said Tracy Serrano, LA Plaza de Cultura y Artes' Interim CEO. “We are excited to offer a welcoming and inclusive space for all to gather and celebrate.” As part of the Casa México Los Ángeles 2026 agenda, the live concert series Summer of Salsa—featuring La Verdad, accompanied by Super DJ Robby—will also take place on Friday, July 10. Attendees at Casa México will be able to visit, throughout the five weeks of the World Cup, the installation by renowned artist Betsabeé Romero, “Tejiendo Redes,” which features a sculptural goal woven with the participation of the community. This binational proposal integrates representations of pre-Hispanic ballgame players and contemporary soccer athletes. Casa México Los Ángeles 2026 is presented by the Consulate General of Mexico, LA Plaza de Cultura y Artes, Telemundo 52, and Jive Live.
