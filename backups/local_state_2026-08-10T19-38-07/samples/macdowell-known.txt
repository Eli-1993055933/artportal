The Art of Hospitality:
Head Chef Erik Johansen on Food, Memory, and Community

Artists arrive at MacDowell carrying the practical necessities of travel and the mental residue of whatever they left behind: unfinished work, long flights, interrupted sleep, ideas still taking shape. Some have crossed continents before stepping into the woods of southern New Hampshire. Others have come only a few hours. Either way, arrival often means disorientation before it means focus, and Head Chef Erik Johansen thinks carefully about how to ease that transition.

MacDowell Awards Spring Summer 2026 Fellowships
For over a century, MacDowell has remained a sanctuary for artistic exploration and achievement.

MacDowell, the nation’s first artist residency program, has awarded Fellowships to visionary artists working across seven disciplines for its Spring Summer 2026 season. These distinguished artists will arrive at MacDowell’s historic campus in Peterborough, New Hampshire between March and August of 2026.

MacDowell Announces Hernan Diaz as Chair of the Board of Directors
A world-renowned writer and MacDowell Fellow, he'll draw upon a wealth of experience and achievement, and a keen awareness of how impactful MacDowell’s residency program can be for artists.

In his role as Board Chair, Diaz will continue to advance MacDowell’s mission to provide transformative support to artists of all backgrounds and disciplines. He will succeed Nell Painter, who has been Chair since 2020.

Made at MacDowell
Eric Chasalow: Tracing Time Through “...arching, reaching, breathless”

Time is the quiet architecture beneath …arching, reaching, breathless, the new release from composer and seven-time MacDowell Fellow Eric Chasalow . It is not simply that the album spans thirty years of creation, from 1997 to 2022, but that it reveals how a life in music builds, ebbs, flows, and transforms.
