About Opportunities

Opportunities

Gasworks’ Residencies Programme was established in 1994 to provide non UK-based artists with the opportunity to live and work in London for a period of up to 3 months.

Gasworks hosts 20 residencies each year in partnership with a variety of institutions, organisations and funders. Residencies are non-prescriptive and process-based, allowing visiting artists to develop projects in response to their new environment, or to conduct research benefiting from London's resources. To date Gasworks has worked with over 600 artists from over 80 countries worldwide, partly through connections with Triangle Network .

Gasworks also offers fellowships through the Triangle Network , providing UK-based artists, researchers and curators with residency opportunities with our Triangle partners worldwide.

Please note that we do not accept speculative applications, join our mailing list if you would like to be updated on residency opportunities.
