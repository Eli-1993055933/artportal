22—24 January 2027

VIP Preview &

Vernissage 21 January

As Southeast Asia’s leading global art fair, ART SG is a dynamic and visionary platform showcasing exceptional contemporary art from the region and around the world.
Applications are now open for the fifth edition of ART SG, presented by Founding and Lead Partner UBS, returning to the Sands Expo and Convention Centre, Marina Bay Sands.

ABOUT OUR 2026 CAMPAIGN

Every year in January, there is a magnetic pull towards the equator. Our 2026 campaign is an open invitation and call to all to join us at ART SG—where we celebrate the dynamic forces that drive artistic creation, cultural exchange and human connection. We bring to life a living space that is constantly evolving, shifting and responding to the world around us.
