The next Open Call for applications to Bemis Center’s Residency Program will tentatively open in early 2028 for residency sessions taking place in 2029.

In 2027 and 2028, residencies will be paused temporarily for capital improvements at Bemis Center, the details of which are forthcoming. We look forward to welcoming artists back to this historic building in 2029 for the forty-eighth year of our international Residency Program!
