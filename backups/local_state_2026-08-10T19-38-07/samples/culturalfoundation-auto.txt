Open Call: Art Residency Programme

The six-month programme provides a platform for professional artists, designers, architects, writers, performers, researchers, film makers and other culture practitioners to advance their skills and practice through dedicated studio spaces and resources. The residency programme enables participants to produce, think and connect with the wider community, through studio visits from a variety of cultural practitioners and a final open studio night.

Applications are open until 31 January 2026 .

Artists in residence are offered:

Dedicated studio space within Cultural Foundation

24 hours access to their studios

Curatorial support

Access to Cultural Foundation facilities

Financial resources

The dates of the Art Residency are:

25 March 2026 – 20 September 2026

25 September 2026 – 20 March 2027

Applicants must state in which half of the year they want to start their residency.

Application Requirements:

A Bachelor&rsquo;s or Master&rsquo;s degree, or a similar academic degree in visual arts or a relevant cultural field

Portfolio with a minimum of 8 works in PDF format

Artist CV, including relevant exhibition history, in PDF format

Contact email and phone number

Short biography (max. 100 words) in PDF format

Artist statement (max. 150 words) in PDF format

Personal photo

Passport copy

Copy of Unified Number (UAE nationals)

Copy of Residency visa (Non-UAE nationals)

Copy of Emirates ID, front and back
