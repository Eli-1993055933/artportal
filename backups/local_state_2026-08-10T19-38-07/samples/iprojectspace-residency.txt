How to apply

Residency length is two to three months long (full calendar months only). Applications are accepted through nine to twelve months in advance. However, feel free to inquire about our availability at any time.

If you are interested in doing a residency at I: project space, please send us the following materials:

- short CV

- portfolio with examples of your previous work

- and most importantly a project proposal

To: [email protected]

Subject: Residency application

Along with these materials, we require an application fee of 25 Euros to consider your application.

Fees and fellowships

I: project space and its residency program operate non-profit, but to cover the costs of maintaining the residency and assistance during the residency we charge a monthly fee. Selected artists will receive all documentation necessary to apply for funding. Occasionally, through certain initiatives and partnerships, we will announce open calls for fully-funded opportunities.
